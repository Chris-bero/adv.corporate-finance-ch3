Key Concepts Review

Payout & Retention Ratios

Payout Ratio: DPS / EPS

Retention Ratio: 1 − Payout Ratio

Dividend Dates

Ex-dividend date: Record date − 2 business days

Dividend Policies

Constant Payout: Fixed % of earnings
Regular: Fixed $ dividend
Low-Regular-Extra: Base + extra when EPS high
Residual: After funding equity needs

Stock Dividends & Splits

New shares: Old shares × Stock dividend %
New price: Old price × Old shares / New shares

Share Repurchases

Shares repurchased: Cash / Price
New EPS: Earnings / (Shares − Repurchased)
